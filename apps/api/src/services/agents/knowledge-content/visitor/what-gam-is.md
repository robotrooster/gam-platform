This booking site belongs to the property you're looking at. GAM is the platform behind it — the software the owner uses to publish availability, take reservations, and run the place.

## The owner sets everything about the property

Rates, deposits, what's on site, the rules, check-in — all of that is the owner's, set by them for this property. GAM holds the booking and takes the payment.

That's why prices and availability here are read live from this property rather than quoted from memory. Two properties on GAM can have completely different rates, deposits and rules, so the only number worth giving you is this property's own.

## What this assistant can do

- Tell you about the property — what it is, where it is, what's on site.
- Give you the current rates for each type of site or unit.
- Check specific dates and come back with a firm total.
- Reserve it for you and hand you a secure link to pay.

## What it won't do

It won't quote you a price it hasn't looked up, and it won't take card details in the chat — payment always happens on a secure checkout page. If a question is the owner's to answer, it says so rather than inventing an answer.
