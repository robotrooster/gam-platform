---
Booking here takes one conversation — you don't fill in a form afterwards or re-type anything you've already said.

## How it goes

1. **Pick a type and give your dates.** Availability is checked live for those exact dates.
2. **You get a firm total** — the stay, the deposit, and tax.
3. **It's read back to you** — the type, the exact check-in and check-out, the total and the deposit — and you say yes.
4. **The site is held and you get a secure link** to pay the deposit.

Nothing is reserved before that yes, and nothing is reserved after it without the link being handed to you.

## You book a type, not a specific site

You choose the kind of site or unit you want; the property assigns the actual one. That's deliberate — it lets the property fit bookings together so more dates stay available, and it means the site you're given matches the type you paid for.

## Payment

Card details are never taken in the chat. The link opens a secure checkout page, and the deposit is paid there. Whatever remains is settled with the property under their terms.

## If your dates aren't available

You'll be told plainly rather than offered something you didn't ask for. Nearby dates or a different type are usually worth asking about.
