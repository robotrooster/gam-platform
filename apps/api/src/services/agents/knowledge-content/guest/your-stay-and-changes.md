title: Your stay and changing it
---
Everything about your reservation — check-in and check-out dates, how many nights, the site or unit, your total, and any note your host left — is on your booking. Ask and it gets read from the record rather than recalled.

## Asking for a change

Three common ones can be handled quickly when the schedule allows:

- **Late checkout**
- **Early check-in**
- **An extra night**

If nothing else is booked into that window, the change is applied straight away and your host is told about it. An extra night extends the booking itself. If the schedule is tight, or you're asking for something else entirely, the request goes to your host to decide.

Either way you'll be told which of the two happened. Nothing is promised before it's actually done — if it says the extra night is yours, it's on the booking.

## What to expect

- Confirm the specifics first — which night, what time — so the request that reaches your host is the one you meant.
- Check-in instructions, gate codes, parking and property rules come from your host.
- A stay change may change your total. Your host settles that with you.

## Anything else

Questions outside your booking — the neighborhood, long-term rentals, anything about the property beyond your stay — go to your host, and they're the right person for them.
