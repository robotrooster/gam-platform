title: What GAM is
---
GAM is the booking and stay platform your host runs their property on. Your reservation, your dates, and any requests you send all live here.

## GAM is the platform, your host runs the property

The host owns the place and makes the calls about it — the rules, the rates, check-in instructions, what's on site and what's allowed. GAM handles the booking itself: holding your reservation, taking payment, and passing your requests to the host.

So when you ask for something about the property, the answer either comes from your booking record or it goes to your host. Nothing about your stay is invented on the spot.

## What this assistant can do for you

- Pull up your stay — dates, nights, the site or unit, and your total.
- Send the host a request: a late checkout, an early check-in, an extra night, or anything else you need.
- Show you what's on site and reserve an amenity for you.

## What it can't

It only sees your one booking. It has no access to other guests, other bookings, or anything on the property beyond your stay — and it never makes a decision that's the host's to make. If it doesn't know something, it says so and passes the question along rather than guessing.
