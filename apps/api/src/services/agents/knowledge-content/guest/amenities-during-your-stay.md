title: Amenities during your stay
---
Properties list what's on site — a pool, a clubhouse, laundry, a fire pit — with their hours, any fee, and whether booking one is instant or needs the host's approval. That list is read from the property, so it reflects what's actually there today.

## Reserving one

1. Ask what's available and you'll get the list with hours and fees.
2. Pick one and give the day and time.
3. You'll be told the fee before anything is booked, and asked for a clear yes.
4. It's reserved — instantly if the amenity allows it, otherwise the host approves it.

## Rules that always apply

- **Your reservation has to fall inside your stay dates.** Nothing can be booked for a day you're not there.
- **Any fee is collected by the property with your stay**, not separately in chat.
- **You'll always hear the fee before you commit.** No amenity is reserved on your behalf without an explicit yes.

If an amenity you were expecting isn't on the list, the host is the one to ask — the list only shows what the property has published.
