---
scope: landlord
title: Waiving a late fee or any other charge
---
**This is yours to do, not the assistant's.** Money coming off a tenant's account is the landlord's own act — the assistant will explain how it works and take you to the right place, but it will not apply a credit for you, the same way it will not waive a charge when a tenant asks it to. Nobody's balance changes because of a conversation.

There is no "waive" button, and nothing on the screening page has anything to do with it. A waiver in GAM is **a credit you apply to the tenant's account** that offsets the charge — the whole amount, or part of it if you only want to forgive some. Crediting $25 against a $50 late fee is an ordinary thing to do.

The charge itself stays on the record. That is deliberate: the history shows the fee was raised and then credited, rather than looking as though it never happened, which is what you want if the tenancy is ever in question later.

The credit lands on their open balance straight away rather than waiting for the next invoice. That matters because rent is paid in full — a forgiven charge left sitting on the balance would block them from paying at all until it cleared.

**Be certain who, before you credit anyone.** Unit numbers repeat across properties: a "204" can exist at more than one, and so can a "12". Confirm the tenant's name and the property first. A credit applied to the wrong tenant is money off the wrong person's balance and a second person still owing a fee they were told was forgiven.
