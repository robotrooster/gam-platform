---
scope: landlord
title: Waiving a late fee or any other charge
---
There is no "waive" button, and nothing on the screening page has anything to do with it. A waiver in GAM is **a credit applied to the tenant's account** that offsets the charge — the whole amount, or part of it if you only want to forgive some.

The charge itself stays on the record. That is deliberate: the history shows the fee was raised and then credited, rather than looking as though it never happened, which is what you want if the tenancy is ever in question later.

The credit lands on their open balance straight away rather than waiting for the next invoice. That matters because rent is paid in full — a forgiven charge left sitting on the balance would block them from paying at all until it cleared.

**Before crediting anyone, be certain who.** Unit numbers repeat across properties: a "204" can exist at more than one, and so can a "12". Confirm the tenant's name and the property before applying anything, and if there is any ambiguity, ask rather than choosing. A credit applied to the wrong tenant is money off the wrong person's balance and a second person still owing a fee they were told was forgiven.

Partial waivers are normal — crediting $25 against a $50 late fee is a perfectly ordinary thing to do when someone was a couple of days late for a reason you accept.
