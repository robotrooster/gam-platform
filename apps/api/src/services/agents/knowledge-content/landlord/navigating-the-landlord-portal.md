title: Finding your way around the landlord portal
---
The GAM landlord portal groups its navigation into these sections. These are the actual section names:

**Overview**
- **Dashboard** — your home overview when you log in.
- **Refer & Earn** — refer other landlords to GAM and earn.
- **Point of Sale** — the POS register for on-site sales at your property.

**Portfolio**
- **Properties** — your properties.
- **Unit Overview** — every unit across your portfolio and its status.
- **Master Schedule** — the booking and stay calendar for your units.
- **Booking Site** — set up and publish your property's public booking website.
- **Tenants** — your tenants.
- **Tenant Onboarding** — moving a new tenant through setup.
- **Leases** — lease agreements, active and past.
- **E-Sign** — documents out for signature and executed records.

**Financials** — one sidebar item that opens to these tabs:
- **Payments** — rent and other payments coming in.
- **Outstanding Balances** — who owes what right now.
- **Rent Roll** — expected rent across your occupied units.
- **Disbursements** — what's paid out to you and how it's split, including on-demand withdrawals of your available balance.
- **Reports** — reporting across your portfolio.
- **Expenses** — record property expenses that feed your profit-and-loss.
- **Bank Feed** — link your operating bank and categorize spending.
- **Bank Reconciliation** — reconcile and categorize bank charges.
- **Banking** — your connected payout bank and identity verification.
- **Lot Rent & Net** — lot rent and investor-operator net (appears when you have mobile-home / lot-rent units).

**Operations**
- **Maintenance** — a hub with three tabs: **Work Orders** (maintenance requests from tenants, triage, and approvals), **Entry Requests** (notice-of-entry to a unit), and **Outages** (service-interruption notices).
- **Inspections** — move-in, move-out, periodic, and turnover inspections.
- **Amenities** — property amenities and their booking rules.
- **Documents** — documents tied to your properties and tenancies.
- **Inventory** — the items you track for your property.
- **Utilities** — meters, readings, and utility billing.
- **Work Trade** — work-trade arrangements with tenants.
- **Surveys** — tenant surveys.

**Screening** — one sidebar item that opens to these tabs:
- **Background Checks** — your applicants' screening results and status.
- **Applicant Pool** — screened applicants looking for housing.
- **Rental History** — applicants' rental history records.

**Admin**
- **Team** — your team members and their per-person permissions.
- **Settings** — account and portfolio settings, including notification preferences.

A few of the financial views (Rent Roll, Expenses, Bank Feed, Bank Reconciliation, Lot Rent & Net) are owner-only, so a team member with limited access may not see them.

Support isn't a sidebar section — it's the floating chat bubble available on every page (it's how you're talking to us right now).

If you can't find something or aren't sure which section to use, tell the support team what you're trying to do and they'll point you to the right place — or handle it directly. Don't guess at a section name; only the ones above exist.
