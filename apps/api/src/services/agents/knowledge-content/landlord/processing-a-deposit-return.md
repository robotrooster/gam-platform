title: Processing a Security Deposit Return
---
The **Deposit Return** page (reached from a lease) reconciles the security deposit at move-out. Most of the math is automatic — you add only the judgment calls.

## What's deducted automatically

- **Move-out lease fees** — fees printed on the lease with a move-out timing (typically the cleaning fee) sum in on their own. Conditional fees ("carpet cleaning within N days, else $X") charge only if the move-out inspection assessed the condition as failed — unassessed or met means no charge, ever.
- **Unpaid balance sweep** — every outstanding unpaid payment on the lease (rent, utilities, late fees, other fees) is pulled into the deduction and settled from the deposit at finalize. You see the exact line items in the "Unpaid balance (auto)" card; to forgive one, mark it paid out-of-band on the Payments page before finalizing.
- **Final utility bills** — a last meter reading entered at move-out becomes a bill that settles straight from the deposit, since it can never ride a monthly invoice.

## What you add

**Damage deductions** are for damage beyond normal wear only. Each line needs a description, a positive amount, and **at least one photo or receipt** as evidence — the page won't finalize without it.

## Interest, refund, and gap

Where state law requires it, **statutory deposit interest** is accrued automatically and added to the tenant's pool — the tenant is owed principal plus interest, minus deductions. If the pool exceeds deductions, the difference is a **refund** that pays out through your normal disbursement path. If deductions exceed the pool, the difference is a **gap** the tenant owes: GAM records it and attempts an auto-charge against the tenant's on-file payment method, flagging you if the charge fails so you can pursue it manually.

## Guardrails

Some unit types require a **finalized in-person move-out walkthrough with photos** before the return can even begin. And when a team member prepares a refund above your approval threshold, it parks as "awaiting landlord approval" — your Finalize is what releases the money.
