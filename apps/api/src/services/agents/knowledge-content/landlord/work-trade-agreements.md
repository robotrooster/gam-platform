title: Setting Up & Managing Work Trade Agreements
---
Work trade lets a tenant work off part of their rent — "rent-for-labor," where hours buy a percent of the monthly bill. The **Work Trade** page shows every agreement with each tenant's hours this month, their target, and any pending logs awaiting your review.

## How the credit works

There are no dollar rates and no per-hour wages. Each agreement has a **monthly hours target**, and every approved hour is worth 1/target of the tenant's **total monthly invoice** (rent plus utilities plus fees):

- Hit the full target → 100% of that month's invoice is covered.
- Work half the target → half the invoice is covered.
- Extra hours beyond the target earn nothing — it's a trade, not paid labor.

The credit applies one month behind: approved hours logged this calendar month reduce the invoice that comes due **next** month. The dollars land on rent first, then spill into utilities and fees only when a near-full target month is worked.

## Targets are per person

Each agreement carries its own hours target, editable inline on its row — different rents and different work don't translate equally, so one tenant's target can differ from another's. The property-level target you set is only the **default applied to new agreements**.

## Creating and running an agreement

An agreement is just the enrollment: tenant, unit, duties, start date, and optionally an end date and renewal terms. The tenant must already have a lease with you. Once active, the tenant logs their hours (date, hours worked, and a description) from their portal; you can also log a substitute entry for them. Each log sits pending until you **approve or reject** it — only approved hours count toward the credit, and a rejection can carry a reason.

Agreements can be **active**, **paused**, or **ended**, and each roster row click-through opens the tenant's lease. Team members need the work-trade permissions to view, manage, and approve.
