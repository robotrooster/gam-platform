title: Adding your properties and units to GAM
---
Getting your portfolio into GAM starts with adding a property, then filling it with units. A property is the building or community itself; units are the individual rentable spaces inside it.

To add a property, give it a name and address and choose its type (for example a standard residential building or an RV community). New properties inherit your account-wide choice of who covers the ACH fee (you can override it per property later); card fees always go to the tenant. ACH payments are a flat $6 per payment, cards run 3.5% plus $0.55 per transaction, and non-US-issued cards add 1.5%. A per-property toggle decides whether the tenant or you absorb the ACH fee; card fees are always paid by the tenant, added on top at checkout. GAM never covers processing fees. You can change the ACH choice later without rebuilding the property.

This is also where you connect the bank account your payouts land in. GAM moves money through Stripe Connect, so you'll go through Stripe's onboarding (identity and banking verification, known as KYC) before payouts can flow. Funds reach you on a schedule once payments clear.

Once the property exists, add units. You add units through a short wizard — a quantity field lets you create a numbered batch of identical units in one pass — pick the property, enter the unit number and details (bedrooms, bathrooms, square footage), set the rent and deposit, then review and create. New units start as vacant, and you're only billed the platform fee on occupied units — $2 per occupied unit per month, with a $10 per-property monthly minimum. Vacant units cost nothing.

With properties and units in place, you're ready to list vacancies and bring tenants on board.
