title: Recording expenses, and why your P&L looks light
---
GAM knows every dollar that came **in** — it collected it. It knows almost
nothing about what went **out**, because most of that never touches the
platform: the plumber you paid in cash, the insurance premium, the truck, the
lumber. Until you record those, your profit-and-loss is income minus the few
costs GAM can see, and it will read higher than the truth.

That is why the P&L says so on its face rather than calling the number "profit".
It is not a bug and it is not a data problem — it is a blank you fill in.

## Where the two screens are

Both sit under **Financials** in the sidebar.

- **Financials → Reports** is where the profit-and-loss lives. Income is broken
  out by rent, fees, utilities, late fees, home/trailer payments, register sales
  and stays, and other income; expenses by the GAM platform fee, maintenance,
  lot rent, and whatever you have entered yourself.
- **Financials → Expenses** is where you enter your own costs, and where the
  ones you have already recorded are listed.

## Recording one

On the Expenses page the control is **Log an expense** — not "add", if you are
hunting for it. It asks for:

- **Scope** — what the cost belongs to, so it lands against the right property
  or unit rather than the portfolio in general.
- **Category** — how it groups on the P&L.
- **Amount** and **Date**.
- **Description** — a line for what it actually was.
- **Receipt** — optional, and worth attaching. It is the difference between a
  number you can defend at tax time and one you cannot.

Saved expenses appear under **Logged expenses** on the same page, and the P&L on
the Reports page picks them up from there.

## Money received or money billed

Every report has one switch at the top: **Money received** (the default) or **Money billed**. The same switch drives the profit-and-loss, owner statements, the rent roll, the dashboard income card and the property reports.

- **Money received** counts money on the day it arrived. Money a tenant pays ahead counts when it arrives, not again when it pays a later bill: one check for two months counts in full in the month you got it, and the next month shows $0 from that tenant. Money still clearing is shown beside the total, not inside it.
- **Money billed** counts each bill in the month it was due, paid or not. "Still owed" is inside the total and "Collected so far" is beside it.

Under both, **a credit you give is never income.** A move-in special, a waived fee or any goodwill credit comes off: under Money received the charge it paid counts only for what the tenant paid in money, and under Money billed it shows as "Credits given". Work trade is never income either; it is shown beside the total.

Register sales, stays and pay links count on their own line, without sales tax or the card fee. Money that never moved through GAM — laundry, interest, anything you file from your bank feed as other income — counts as "Other income".

The property report leads with income by category: lot or space rent, each utility by type (electric, water, sewer, trash, propane and the rest), late fees, home payments, other fees, register sales, and stays and pay links. Each shows billed against collected under the switch. Then come expenses — GAM's platform fee, maintenance, lot rent and what you entered — then the net. The unit-by-unit lists are below it, folded until you open them.

## What GAM already counts without being told

The platform fee, and maintenance and lot rent where those moved through GAM.
Everything else is yours to enter. If your P&L shows a suspiciously healthy net,
the usual reason is simply that a year of real costs has not been entered yet.
