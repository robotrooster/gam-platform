scope: landlord
title: Posting service interruption (outage) notices
---
When a utility goes down — or planned work will take one down — post a service interruption notice so every affected resident hears immediately, from you, instead of piecemeal through calls to the office. Notices live in the maintenance area of the landlord portal under the Outages tab, and you can also ask the support assistant to post one for you.

## What a notice covers

Each notice names the affected service (water, power, gas, heat/AC, elevator, internet, parking, or other), when the interruption starts, and — optionally — when you expect service back, a short headline, and a message with detail. You can mark it as an emergency for an unplanned outage, which changes the framing residents see. A notice can cover the whole property or target specific units (unit-level targeting is done in the portal; notices posted through the assistant are property-wide).

## What happens when you post

- Residents in the affected units are notified immediately — once, at post time.
- Team members who work the property (anyone with the Outages permission) and the owner are alerted too, so the front desk hears directly.
- A notice scheduled for the future posts as "scheduled" and flips to "active" automatically when its start time arrives. Residents are NOT re-notified at the flip — the post-time notice is the notification.

## Resolving a notice

Resolution is always a manual step — GAM never auto-resolves at the expected-restore time, because that time is an estimate and a false "service restored" signal is worse than none. When service is actually back, mark the notice resolved and choose whether to send residents an all-clear notification. Only send the all-clear once you've confirmed the service is really restored.

If a notice was posted by mistake or the work is called off, cancel it instead — cancelling doesn't send any restoration message.
