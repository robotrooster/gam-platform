Your GAM account controls your payouts, your bank connection and your tenants' records, so it's worth a few minutes of care. If you've forgotten your password you can reset it yourself.

## Resetting it

1. Go to the landlord sign-in page.
2. Select **Forgot Password**.
3. Enter the email on your account.
4. Follow the reset link in your inbox and choose a new password.

The link goes to the email on file. If it hasn't arrived in a few minutes, check spam and confirm you typed the right address.

## Everyday habits

- **Use a strong, unique password.** Don't reuse one from another site, and make it long. A password manager makes this painless.
- **Keep your email secure.** It's the recovery path for your GAM account — whoever controls it can request resets. Give it its own strong password.
- **Sign out on shared devices.**
- **Be cautious with links.** GAM only sends a reset link when you ask for one from the Forgot Password page. Treat any other message asking for your login as suspect.
- **Give your team the narrowest role that works.** Team members can be limited by role and by property, so a maintenance lead doesn't need access to your books.

## If something looks wrong

Reach out right away if you see activity you don't recognize, can't sign in, have lost access to your account email, or think someone else has your password.

Account security and access always go to a human GAM Strategist, who can help you recover safely. Don't keep retrying if you're locked out — ask to be connected.
