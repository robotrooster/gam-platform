The assistant is built into your landlord portal. Open it from anywhere and ask in plain language — it reads your own book, so you get your figures rather than a general explanation.

## What it's good at

- **Your rent roll and balances** — who's behind and by how much, what's outstanding across a property.
- **Portfolio questions** — occupancy, average lease length, how often rent arrives late, how long repairs take to close, turnover.
- **Rankings** — longest tenancy, most maintenance requests filed and what they cost, most complaints, biggest balance owed.
- **Your P&L** — income and expenses over a period. It uses the same calculation as your reports page, so the numbers agree.
- **Leases and expirations** — what's ending and when.
- **Complaints** — what tenants have filed and what's still open.

## Two things to know

When a figure isn't recorded, it says so instead of estimating. If no expenses have been entered, it will give you income but refuse to call the result profit — because it wouldn't be.

And when you give it a partial name or a bare unit number, it confirms who it landed on before handing over someone's financial standing: "that's Bob Chen in Apt 101 — different Chen?" That's deliberate. This is the number a notice gets served over.

Ask specifically and it does better: "which units are more than 30 days behind" beats "show me my tenants."
