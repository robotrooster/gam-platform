title: Reservations, the Master Schedule & Your Property's Booking Website
---
Short-term stays run through two connected surfaces: your property's **public booking website**, where guests reserve and pay a deposit, and the **Master Schedule** page in your portal, where every stay lands.

## The booking website

Each property can publish its own booking site — you configure it from the **Booking Site** page in your portal (also reachable as the Booking Page tab on the Master Schedule): a web address (slug), welcome text, stay rates, deposit rules, contact info and office hours, and gallery photos. Nothing is public until you tick **Publish**.

Guests pick their **dates first**, then choose from the site types actually open for those dates. Every stay is charged the lowest price for its nights: the cheapest mix of whole months, whole weeks and nights that covers the stay (six nights can cost the same as a week), and a stay is never prorated. The booking site, the schedule, pay links and the register all charge that same price. Your short-term lodging tax is applied to stays under 30 nights (30+ is tax-exempt). Guests book a site **type**, never a specific site: the system assigns the best-fit spot, and the guest learns their site number the morning of check-in. If dates are full, guests can join a waitlist and get a timed claim link when a spot opens.

## Deposits

Short stays (under 30 nights) pay a **percentage of the stay total** as a deposit through Stripe checkout — the booking is a tentative hold until the deposit clears. A guest booking 30 nights or more online chooses a **lease** or a **stay** (below). A **stay** is paid **one calendar month at a time** at the monthly rate, never prorated: the booking covers the first month, and each later month is added with **Add a month**, which extends the same stay. A **lease** pays its deposit at booking, and its rent is then billed by your property's rent-due setting — prorated for the first partial month only when rent is due on a fixed day of the month; when rent is due on the move-in day, there's no proration.

## The master schedule

The Master Schedule's timeline shows every unit's bookings and leases on one grid. You can drag a reservation to another unit or date, drag its edges to change check-in/check-out, and work from the list, reservations, and history tabs. Guest-submitted change requests appear for your approve/decline.

## Checking a guest out

Checking a guest out — on the day, or early — is done on the Master Schedule: open the stay and press **Check out**. When a guest leaves before the booked day, that window asks one question about the money (keep the price as booked or charge only the nights stayed; or, if they paid more than the nights stayed are worth, no refund, refund the unused nights, or a different amount) and sends any refund back the way it was paid. If a long stay has a lease, it ends on the day they leave. The assistant cannot check a guest out or decide anything about the money — point the person to the schedule's Check out button.

## Long stays: screening, and lease or stay

Back-to-back stays by the same person at the same property count as one continuous stay.

- **More than three weeks (22+ nights):** the guest needs a background check before check-in. If they have none on file with you, the check's fee is added to the payment that books or extends the stay, and they're emailed the link — already paid. That fee is GAM's: paid by card or online, GAM keeps it and it never reaches your payout; paid in cash, by check or on a charge account at your counter, you're holding it, so it comes out of your next payout. Check-in waits until the results are back and you've approved or denied it; after a denial you may still check them in.
- **30 nights or more:** the stay is either a **lease** or a **stay**. A lease holds the site for as long as they stay; a stay holds it only through the time they've paid for. Online, the guest chooses; at the register, on a pay link or on the schedule, your staff choose. Either way you're notified. A lease choice drafts a month-to-month lease on your Leases page for you to review and send; nothing is drafted otherwise. A lease chosen partway through a stay starts that day, and what was already paid for the nights from then on comes off its first bill. A stay with no lease pays its site's utilities, billed to the guest's own GAM login by their email.
- **A returning guest skips the check.** When you add or edit a reservation on the schedule, sell the stay at the register or send it on a pay link, an owner (or a manager allowed to invite tenants) can choose **Returning guest — they've stayed with us before**: no check, no fee, and check-in doesn't wait. It's recorded on the stay as your confirmation, and it draws on the same yearly returning-resident allowance as the residents you invite back (counted once per person per year — the same person again that year is free). When the allowance is used up the choice is greyed out. The booking website never offers it.
- **Work trade on a stay.** Tick **Work trade** on a new reservation (or its edit) and the guest works off what it covers — everything by default; untick anything they should pay for. Choose monitored (you approve their hours) or trusted, and the hours follow the property's work-trade setting. Covered rent makes the site's price $0, so there's nothing to collect for it; covered utilities aren't billed. The trade follows the stay's dates and site, ends when they check out or the stay is cancelled, and carries into a lease drafted from the stay. The site still counts toward GAM's monthly fee.
- **Approving a stay guest's check** only clears them for check-in, once the results are back. It doesn't put them on the site as a resident or draft a lease — a guest who chose a lease already has their draft lease from the stay.
