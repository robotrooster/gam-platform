title: Reservations, the Master Schedule & Your Property's Booking Website
---
Short-term stays run through two connected surfaces: your property's **public booking website**, where guests reserve and pay a deposit, and the **Master Schedule** page in your portal, where every stay lands.

## The booking website

Each property can publish its own booking site — you configure it from the **Booking Site** page in your portal (also reachable as the Booking Page tab on the Master Schedule): a web address (slug), welcome text, stay rates, deposit rules, contact info and office hours, and gallery photos. Nothing is public until you tick **Publish**.

Guests pick their **dates first**, then choose from the site types actually open for those dates. Pricing auto-tiers by length of stay — under 7 nights bills nightly, 7–29 nights weekly, 30+ nights monthly, prorated for odd lengths — with your short-term lodging tax applied to stays under 30 nights (30+ is tax-exempt). Guests book a site **type**, never a specific site: the system assigns the best-fit spot, and the guest learns their site number the morning of check-in. If dates are full, guests can join a waitlist and get a timed claim link when a spot opens.

## Deposits

Short stays (under 30 nights) pay a **percentage of the stay total** as a deposit through Stripe checkout — the booking is a tentative hold until the deposit clears. Monthly-tier stays (30+ nights) owe a **flat deposit** instead (per-property, $150 by default), always capped at one month's rent. Monthly quotes show a calendar-aligned invoice plan and can note "plus utilities" if you bill utilities back on long stays.

## The master schedule

The Master Schedule's timeline shows every unit's bookings and leases on one grid. You can drag a reservation to another unit or date, drag its edges to change check-in/check-out, and work from the list, reservations, and history tabs. Guest-submitted change requests appear for your approve/decline.

## Long stays become leases

Anyone staying **30 or more nights** (7+, if the property runs weekly leases) gets a **draft lease created automatically**. You're notified with the guest's GAM history — prior stays, any approved background check, and whether they've had continuous tenancy since — and you decide: screen first, or send the lease directly if you know them. The system never auto-sends a background check.
