title: Creating & Running Unit Inspections
---
Inspections document a unit's condition with itemized checklists, photos, and walkthrough videos. Create one from the Inspections page with **New Inspection**.

## The four types

- **Move-in** — documents condition at the start of a tenancy.
- **Move-out** — documents condition at the end. It automatically compares against the unit's latest finalized move-in inspection: any item whose condition got worse (the ordering is good, fair, damaged, missing) counts as damage.
- **Periodic** — a routine check during a tenancy. These are tenant-submitted: the tenant photographs the unit themselves from their portal.
- **Turnover** — clean/repair work between tenancies.

When you create an inspection you pick the type and the unit — the tenant and lease attach automatically from the unit, so a vacant unit simply has neither (normal for turnover and periodic). A standard checklist is seeded from the unit's type and bedroom count, and the in-app assistant can run the whole walkthrough hands-free if you prefer.

## Who signs what

The tenant signs the **move-in inspection only** — their certification of the photos and documented conditions. Periodic inspections need no tenant signature: the tenant submits them from their own logged-in portal, which is their attestation, and the submission lands in your queue as "Submitted for review." Move-out and turnover inspections are staff-conducted, so your (or your inspector's) signature alone is enough. Videos, once uploaded, are permanent — there is intentionally no way to delete them.

## Finalizing

Finalizing is a landlord action and locks in the record. For a move-out, the comparison against move-in is computed at finalize. Any conditional lease fees linked to "Lease conditions" checklist items are assessed at the same time — good or fair means the condition is met and nothing is charged; damaged or missing fails it and the fee joins the deposit-return sweep.

If a tenant-submitted periodic inspection looks off, you can flag it instead of passing it: the submitted record closes (photos kept read-only), and an in-person inspection is auto-scheduled three business days out with the tenant's photos linked for side-by-side review. The tenant only sees neutral copy — the flag reason stays on your side.
