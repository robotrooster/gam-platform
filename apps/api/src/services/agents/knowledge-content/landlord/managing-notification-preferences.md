GAM tells you what's happening across your book — a rent payment landing, a maintenance request coming in, an application submitted, a lease nearing its end — so you're not checking the portal all day. You control which of those reach you.

## Adjusting them

1. Sign in to your landlord portal.
2. Open your notification settings.
3. Turn individual types on or off.
4. Save.

Your preferences are yours alone. They don't change what your tenants receive, and they don't change what your team members receive — each person sets their own.

## Good to know

- Some account-critical messages, like security and access alerts, still send even with optional notifications off, so you don't miss something that matters.
- Notifications are a convenience layer. The underlying records always live in the portal, so nothing is lost if you turn one off.
- Turning off a notification does not turn off the underlying thing. Silencing maintenance alerts doesn't pause the requests; they keep arriving in the queue.

If an alert mentions something you don't recognize, or you're not getting ones you expect, reach out and we'll sort it out.
