scope: landlord
title: Approving or declining an applicant
---
Once a background check is complete, you make the call. GAM records your decision and notifies the applicant — but the decision itself is always yours.

## When you can decide

You can approve or decline a check once it has results back from the provider — that is, when its status is **complete**. A decision can't be recorded before then: not while the check is still **submitted** or **processing**, nor on one that is pending, awaiting the applicant, failed, cancelled, expired, or already decided. A later update from the screening provider never changes a decision you've made.

## Recording the decision

From the application detail, choose **Approve** or **Decline**. You can attach notes explaining your reasoning. Once recorded:

- The decision and timestamp are saved against the check, along with who decided it.
- The applicant is emailed the outcome. An approval email includes any notes you added; a decline email stays neutral.
- An **approval** marks the check valid for six months, so you have a window to move toward a lease without re-screening.

## After a decline

When you decline an applicant whose check ran through a screening provider, you're prompted to send them an adverse-action notice. You write it — GAM hands you the screening agency's contact details and your saved template to start from, then emails your notice to the applicant and keeps a copy on file for the check. You can also choose to handle the notice yourself outside GAM. This is legal-adjacent: exactly what is required, what it must say, and any waiting periods depend on your local laws, and the notice is yours — check your local laws or your own counsel if you are unsure.

## Sending the notice again

A check keeps one notice on file. If you send it again, the copy on file is replaced with the one you sent last.

## Guests screened for a stay

A guest staying more than three weeks (22 nights or more) is screened before check-in. You can approve or deny their check once the results are back — not before. Either one clears the stay for check-in (after a denial you may still check them in). It doesn't put them on the site as a resident or draft a lease: a guest who chose a lease for their stay already has that lease drafted on your Leases page, and approval never drafts a second one.

## What approval does next

Apart from a guest screened for a stay (above), approving a check that names a space drafts the lease for that space from its default lease template, with the applicant on it and the move-in date and term they gave, and sends it to you to sign first. If the check names no space, you're asked to pick one. Approval doesn't move anyone in by itself — the signed lease does.
