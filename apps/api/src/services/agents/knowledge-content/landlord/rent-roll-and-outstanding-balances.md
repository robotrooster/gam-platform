title: Reading Your Rent Roll & Outstanding Balances
---
GAM gives you two complementary money views: the **Rent Roll** (every household, what each was charged, paid and still owes for a month, and how on time they pay) and **Outstanding Balances** (who owes right now, for the front desk).

## The rent roll

The Rent Roll is grouped in **property folders** (a single property opens by itself). Each folder header shows the property's occupied spaces and the picked month's counts — how many households paid on time, paid late, paid part, or still owe.

Each row is one **household** (everyone on a lease together):
- **Name and space(s)** — click the name to open that household's full payment report.
- **Lease rent** — the rent the active lease bills each month, never the unit's listed rent.
- **Charged** — everything billed on the month's bills, late fees included (with an "incl. $X late fees" note).
- **Paid** — money paid (and account credit applied); work trade is shown beside it, never counted as money.
- **Owed** — what is still owed on the month's bills.
- **Status** — a color with words: green paid on time, yellow paid in part, orange paid in full but late, red nothing paid past the grace period (also delinquent or eviction-mode spaces), gray "Due" while the month is not past its grace period yet.
- **Payment health** — the share of months paid on time since the household's first bill on GAM (all time). A month counts once it is past its grace period; a month work trade covered in full counts as on time; late fees never decide a month; the first month moved onto GAM only counts if it was on time.
- **A strip of monthly stamps** — every month since the household's first bill on GAM, newest first; it scrolls sideways. Each month's header counts how many households were late.

Households on an occupied space are always on the page; a household that moved out stays on it while it was billed in the last 12 months.

The arrow beside a name opens that month's charges: what each is for (Rent, Electric, Late fee, ...), when and how it was paid, and days past grace.

**Lease rent / month** at the top totals the active leases' rent, with rent traded for work shown apart. It is the same figure as the dashboard's Expected Monthly Rent card, which opens this page.

### Days late

"Days late" always means **days past the grace period**. Rent due the 1st with a 5-day grace period is late on the 6th — the day late fees start — so a payment on the 6th is 1 day past grace. Grace comes from the lease, else the property, else 5 days.

## Outstanding balances

The Outstanding Balances page is the front-desk "who owes" view. Each row is a person with an unpaid balance: the amount owed, the months it is for, the oldest due date (with a days-past-grace tag), and their phone and email so someone at the counter knows who to call. Payments still clearing are not counted as owed.

## Team access

The Rent Roll and the payment reports open for the account owner and for team members with the **View all payments** permission; staff assigned to particular properties see only those properties, and totals across people (and the Owed column on the Reports pages) show only to owners and property managers. A bookkeeper sees the Reports overview's Outstanding Balance and the Owed column for the properties they keep. Outstanding Balances requires the balances-view grant (part of the Front Desk preset), and staff locked to specific properties only see balances at those properties.
