title: Reading Your Rent Roll & Outstanding Balances
---
GAM gives you two complementary money views: the **Rent Roll** (what your leases say you should collect each month) and **Outstanding Balances** (who actually owes right now).

## The rent roll

The Rent Roll page shows one row per **occupied unit**, grouped by property, with the tenant, lease start and end dates (or "Month-to-month"), the unit's status, and its contracted monthly rent. The **Expected Monthly Rent** total at the top uses the exact same formula as the dashboard KPI, so the two always agree.

### Why non-paying and evicting units still count

Occupied means **active, delinquent, and suspended** units. That's deliberate: rent owed comes from the lease, not from payment behavior. A tenant who has stopped paying, or one you're evicting, still owes rent under their signed lease — so their unit stays on the roll and their rent stays in the expected total. Removing them would understate what you're contractually owed. Only genuinely vacant units are excluded.

## Outstanding balances

The Outstanding Balances page is the front-desk "who owes" view. Each row is a tenant with an unpaid balance: the amount owed, how many open invoices make it up, the oldest due date (with a days-overdue tag), and the tenant's phone and email so someone at the counter knows who to call.

"Outstanding" here means unpaid invoice balance — each invoice's total minus the settled payments applied to it, across invoices that are still pending or partially paid. The total owed appears at the top of the page.

## Team access

Both pages are permission-gated for staff. Outstanding Balances requires the balances-view grant (part of the Front Desk preset), and staff locked to specific properties only see balances at those properties. The rent roll is a financial view, gated the same way as the dashboard financial rollup.
