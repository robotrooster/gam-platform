The **Utilities** page runs monthly meter readings and per-unit utility billing for a property. Meters can be individual **sub-meters** (billed on the exact reading) or **RUBS master meters** (a usage total allocated across units); meters billed straight to you as the landlord don't generate tenant reading runs. Sewer has no meter of its own — it rides the water meter, so one water reading bills both at their own rates.

## Reading runs

A **reading run** opens automatically on the last business day of each month (you can also start one early). The guided walk steps meter to meter, one unit at a time. Entry is deliberately **blind**: the reader is never shown the previous reading, so nothing biases what they type. Sub-meter reads are entered as the full odometer value at the meter's digit width, leading zeros included.

When the last meter is read, the run moves to a short **verification walk** — a blind re-read of a system-picked list of meters — and billing waits for it. Once verification finishes, bills generate automatically and each charge rides that tenant's next monthly invoice. Spots without a responsible lease record a reading only, with no charge.

## The double-check queue

Suspicious entries — a read below last month's, or unusually high usage — are **silently flagged**: the person entering the read sees nothing different, and the flagged charge is held from billing. Flags land in your **Readings to double-check** queue, where you (unlike the reader) see both values. You either correct the value or confirm it; a genuine low read must be explained as a **meter rollover** (bills the wrap-around usage) or a **meter swap/reset** (no charge that cycle). An unread meter holds only that unit's invoice — the **Complete now** button closes a stuck run, and unread or flagged meters simply produce no bill that cycle.

## Propane and taxes

Propane is billed per tank fill: gallons times the price per gallon you set on each fill, with optional 2- or 4-payment splits (property opt-in, with gallon minimums you control). A new fill makes any prior fill balance due immediately. You can also set per-utility **tax rates** per property — tax shows as a separate amount on each charge, and changing a rate never rewrites past bills.
