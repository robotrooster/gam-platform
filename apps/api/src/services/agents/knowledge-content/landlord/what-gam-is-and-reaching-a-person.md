GAM is the software you run your rental operation on: rent collection and payouts, leases and signing them through GoldSign, maintenance, screening, inspections, books, and the record of all of it in one place.

## GAM is the platform — the property decisions stay yours

GAM does not own your properties, set your rents, write your lease terms, or decide a repair. You do. GAM gives you the tools and the record, moves the money, and holds the audit trail. When a tenant asks the assistant about a repair or a lease term, it routes them to you rather than answering for you — because that call is yours to make.

## Three doors when you need help

- **This assistant** — the fastest one. It reads your own book, so you get your figures rather than a general explanation: your rent roll, who's behind, your P&L, your occupancy.
- **GAM support** — platform and account problems the assistant can't resolve, or any time you'd rather talk to a person.
- **Your own team** — anything you've delegated to a manager or maintenance lead lives in their queue, scoped by the permissions you gave them.

## What always goes to a person

Some things are handed to a human GAM Strategist no matter what:

- Moving or refunding money
- Account security, access, or sign-in problems

Just ask to be connected and we'll bring one in.
