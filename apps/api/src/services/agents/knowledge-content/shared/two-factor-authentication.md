title: Setting up two-factor authentication
---
Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a one-time code to your sign-in, so a stolen password alone isn't enough to get into your account. It's optional for tenant and landlord accounts, and worth turning on.

## Setting it up

Enrollment starts from the Security area of your portal. You'll see three steps:

1. **Scan the QR code** with any authenticator app — Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password, and Bitwarden all work. If you can't scan, there's a link to add the account manually.
2. **Save your recovery codes.** You get 10 one-time codes, shown only once — they are not retrievable later. Store them somewhere safe, like a password manager. You'll confirm you've saved them before you can finish.
3. **Enter the current 6-digit code** from your app to confirm. From your next sign-in on, 2FA is active.

## Signing in with 2FA

After entering your email and password, you'll be asked for the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. The code screen is short-lived — if you wait too long, you'll be asked to sign in again.

## If you lose your authenticator

Enter one of your saved recovery codes in place of the 6-digit code at sign-in. Each recovery code works exactly once. Once you're back in, you can disable 2FA (this requires your password) and re-enroll with a new device, which also generates a fresh set of recovery codes.

If you've lost both your authenticator app and your recovery codes, there is no self-service reset — contact GAM support to recover access securely.

## Turning it off

You can disable 2FA any time from the Security page. You'll need to re-enter your password to confirm — that's deliberate, so a stolen session can't quietly remove your second factor.
