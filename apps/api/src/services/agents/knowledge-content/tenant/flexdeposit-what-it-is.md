title: FlexDeposit — who holds the money and what cancelling means
---
If you are on FlexDeposit, **GAM holds your deposit, not your landlord.** It sits in our custody for the length of your lease. Your landlord's books show the deposit as covered, but the actual money is with us, so anything about the deposit itself is ours to answer — asking your landlord to change it will not get you anywhere, because they are not holding it.

You are funding your own deposit in instalments. GAM is not lending you anything and there is no debt: if you stop paying, nobody is coming after you, and what you have paid in is still yours.

**Cancelling.** The instalment plan is not something you can switch off part-way through a lease. What you can do is **pay the outstanding balance in full**, which finishes the plan early — after that there are no more instalments to pay. The **$3 a month custody fee continues for the rest of the lease term** either way, because we are still holding the money for you. The arrangement genuinely ends when you move out.

**At move-out**, the balance comes back to you, less anything owed to your landlord for damage beyond normal wear. If you move to another GAM property, the deposit is forwarded to the new place rather than refunded and re-collected.

If you want to pay the balance off, or you are moving out and want to know what comes back, ask and we will pull up your actual figures.
