title: How your security deposit works
---
Your security deposit is money you pay at the start of your lease, held in case there are charges to settle when you move out. On GAM, the deposit is part of your lease terms: the amount is set by your landlord in the lease you sign, listed as a move-in item alongside your first month's rent and any other move-in fees.

## Who sets the amount

GAM is the software platform that runs your payments and your lease record. We don't set your deposit, charge it, or decide what it covers. Your landlord sets the deposit amount in the lease, and you can see it on your lease details before you sign. If the figure ever looks wrong to you, your landlord is the right person to ask, because they control that field.

## How it's paid and held

You pay your deposit electronically, the same way you pay rent on GAM, by bank transfer (ACH) or card. There's no cash or check option. Depending on a setting your landlord chooses for the property, a small processing fee may be added to your payment or covered by your landlord.

When you pay your deposit through GAM, GAM holds it for you in a separate trust account — kept apart from your landlord's own money. Your landlord sees it credited to your unit but can't spend it during your lease, and it's never applied to your monthly rent; the funds stay safely in GAM's custody until move-out. (If you were already renting from your landlord before they joined GAM, your landlord may still be holding your original deposit directly — your deposit details show who holds yours.)

## Deposit interest

In some places, a held deposit earns interest that's owed back to you, and the rules differ from one location to another. When GAM holds your deposit, it automatically tracks any interest your location requires and adds it to what you're owed at move-out — you don't have to do anything. (If your landlord is holding your deposit directly, any interest is handled by your landlord under your lease and local law.) Whether interest applies at all, and how it's calculated, depends on your location, so check your local rules for the specifics.

## At move-out

When your lease ends, your landlord reviews the deposit and enters any deductions for unpaid charges or damage beyond normal wear. GAM then settles it: your refund is returned to you, and any retained portion is released to your landlord. If the deductions come to more than your deposit, GAM records the difference as a balance you owe. See "Getting your deposit back at move-out" for how that settlement works step by step.

Your deposit and its activity are always visible in your GAM account, so you have a clear record from the day you pay it.
