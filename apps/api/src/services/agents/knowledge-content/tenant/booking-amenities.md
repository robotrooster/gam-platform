title: Booking amenities at your property
---
If your property has reservable shared spaces — a clubhouse, pool, or pavilion — you can book them for private use from the Amenities section of your tenant portal.

Each amenity card shows what you need to know before you book: capacity, any reservation fee, open hours, the maximum reservation length, and whether booking is instant or needs management approval. You'll also see upcoming windows when the space is already taken.

## How to reserve

Pick the amenity, choose a start and end time, and optionally add a title (like "Birthday party") and a guest count. If the area reserves instantly, the time is yours right away as long as it's free. If it requires approval, your request goes to management and you'll be notified of the decision.

A few limits can apply, depending on how your property configured the space: a maximum number of hours per reservation, a cap on how far in advance you can book, and a per-person monthly reservation limit.

## Fees and cancellations

Some areas carry a reservation fee, which is billed to your account once the reservation is live and paid through your normal payment methods. Weekend dates (Friday through Sunday) may use a different rate if your property set one. You can cancel from the "My reservations" list. Cancel 48 hours or more before the start time and the fee is refundable — an unpaid fee is simply voided, and a paid fee is flagged for refund. Inside 48 hours, the fee stands.

## Private events

Some areas also allow private events. Booking an event may require a deposit that locks the space in — that deposit is non-refundable by design. Once it's paid, everyone at the property is notified the space is reserved. If the deposit isn't paid by the start time, the space opens back up.
