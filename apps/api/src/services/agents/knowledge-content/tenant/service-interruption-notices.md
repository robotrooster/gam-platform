scope: tenant
title: Service interruption notices — when a utility is down
---
When a utility at your property is interrupted — water, power, gas, heat or AC, the elevator, internet, or parking access — your property posts a service interruption notice. You get a notification when it's posted, and any live notice also shows as a banner in your tenant portal so you can see at a glance what's down and what to expect.

## What the notice tells you

Each notice says which service is affected, when the interruption starts, and — when the property has an estimate — when service is expected back. The expected-restore time is an estimate from your property, not a guarantee; it isn't updated minute-by-minute. A notice may also be marked as an emergency for an unplanned outage, or carry a message from your property with more detail (what happened, what to do in the meantime).

Notices can be posted ahead of time for planned work (for example, a water shutoff for a repair scheduled tomorrow morning). You're notified when it's posted, so you have warning before the interruption starts.

## When service comes back

Your property marks the interruption resolved once service is actually restored, and may send an all-clear notification. If the banner is still showing but you believe service is back, it usually just means the property hasn't marked it resolved yet.

## If something is down and there's no notice

A notice only exists when your property posts one. If a utility seems out and there's no notice in your portal, report it by filing a maintenance request — that routes the problem to your property right away. For an emergency like a burst pipe or a gas smell, use the emergency priority on the request, and for gas follow your local utility's safety guidance first.
