title: Managing your notification preferences
---
GAM keeps you in the loop about your rental — payment reminders, maintenance updates, messages from your landlord, and more. You decide how each kind of notification reaches you, so you hear about what matters without the noise you don't want.

You'll find these controls in your notification preferences. They're organized by type of notification, and for each type you can turn two delivery channels on or off independently:

- Email — sent to the address on your account.

A third channel, **in-app** (the alerts you see inside GAM, with an unread count), is always on and can't be turned off — it's how the app makes sure nothing slips by. The email toggle is separate per type, so you can fine-tune things to your liking — for example, keep email on for payment reminders but off for routine updates. GAM does not send text messages. Flip whichever toggles fit how you prefer to be reached; your choices save right away and apply going forward.

Inside the app, your notifications collect in one place. You can mark a notification as read by clicking it — that just tidies your list; it doesn't delete the underlying update.

A couple of things worth knowing: keep your email address accurate so messages actually reach you. And some essential account messages — like a password reset you requested — are always delivered regardless of your preferences, because they're tied to keeping your account secure.

GAM is the platform behind your rental account, not your landlord, but these preferences are how we make sure the right updates find you the right way.
