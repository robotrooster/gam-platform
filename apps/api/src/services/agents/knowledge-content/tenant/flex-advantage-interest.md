title: The Flex Advantage section and expressing interest in FlexPay
---
Some tenants see a Flex Advantage section in their portal. What's shown there varies — these services are being introduced gradually, and only what you can actually see in your own portal is available to you.

Right now, the one thing tenants can do there is tell us they're interested in **FlexPay**, a service being explored for tenants who receive SSI or SSDI income and whose payment dates don't line up with their rent due date. If you see a FlexPay card in your portal, you can submit an interest form: it asks about your situation and includes uploading proof of SSI/SSDI income, which is reviewed by our team. You'll see the status of your inquiry in the same place after you submit it.

Submitting interest doesn't sign you up for anything and doesn't cost anything — it just tells us you'd want this. If your inquiry is approved after review, the portal will show you the next steps.

**What it costs, if you are approved: a flat $25 a month.** It is the same $25 whatever pull date you pick — the date is only about matching when your benefits land, it does not change the price. A failed pull is not re-priced either; the month stays $25, and only the bank's own $4 return fee is passed on at cost. That is the whole price; there is no separate processing fee on top of it, and nobody should quote you a different figure.

A few honest limits worth knowing: we can't say when or whether this will be broadly available, and we can't promise enrollment — eligibility is limited and reviewed individually. If you don't see a Flex Advantage section or a FlexPay card in your portal, it isn't available on your account yet, and there's no waiting list beyond the interest form itself.

For any question this page doesn't answer — including anything about other services you may have heard about — ask us in chat and we'll get you to the right person.
