---
scope: tenant
title: Paying a utility bill when you don't rent from this landlord
---
Some people use this portal without renting anything here. If a neighboring property supplies your trash, water, or electric — a shared can, a submeter, a line that runs to your building — the person who supplies it can bill you through GAM, and you pay it here.

Your account works the same way a renter's does, with less in it. You'll see what you owe, what the meter read and over what dates, and a button to pay. There's no rent, no lease, and no deposit.

**One bill, one payment.** If you're on more than one utility from the same supplier — trash and electric, say — that's a single invoice and a single payment, not one per utility.

You are only billed once you've agreed to it. Either you accepted an invite by email, or the supplier confirmed you'd already agreed to the arrangement offline. If you don't believe you agreed to any of it, say so to whoever supplies the utility — nothing should be issued to you otherwise.

Late fees follow the supplying property's normal policy, and because there's no rent involved, a policy written as a percentage is applied to the utilities instead.

**If you're moving out**, there's a button that tells them so. It asks them to come take a final meter reading so your last bill is accurate. It does not close your account or cancel what you already owe — anything outstanding stays owed.

If the property you actually rent from later joins GAM, these charges quietly fold into your regular bill. You'd see one document with one due date instead of two. Nothing you have to do, and nothing about what you owe changes.
