The assistant is built into your GAM account. Open it from anywhere and type what you're wondering about — no digging through menus, no waiting on a reply.

## What it's good at

It's account-aware, which is the point: it looks at your own record and answers with your numbers instead of a general explanation.

- **Payments** — "what do I owe right now?", "when is my next payment due?", "why was there a fee on my last payment?"
- **Lease** — "where's my signed lease?", "what's my lease end date?", "how much is my pet deposit?"
- **Maintenance** — "how do I file a request?", "what's the status of the one I sent?"
- **Notifications** — turning reminders on or off for a specific topic.

## Two things to know

Anything that depends on your specific lease terms gets looked up in your lease rather than answered generally, because those terms differ by property, by landlord, and by state — that's why it checks instead of generalizing.

And if your question needs a person, it hands you off to the GAM support team rather than leaving you stuck.

The more specific your question, the better the answer: "why is my balance higher this month?" beats "help."
