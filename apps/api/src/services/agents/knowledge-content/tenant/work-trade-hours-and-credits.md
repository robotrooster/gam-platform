title: Logging work trade hours and how credits apply
---
Some properties offer work trade: you do agreed-upon work around the property, and the hours you put in reduce your monthly bill. Your landlord sets up the agreement — it lists your duties, the term, and your monthly hours target. You'll find it all under **Work Trade** in your portal's sidebar, where you log your hours and watch your credit build.

## How the credit works

Work trade is a percent model, not an hourly wage. Each approved hour covers 1/target of your total monthly bill — rent plus utilities plus fees. Work the full target in a month and next month's bill is fully covered; work half the target and half is covered. The credit is capped at 100% of the bill — extra hours beyond the target don't pay out as cash.

Timing matters: hours you work (and get approved) in one calendar month reduce the invoice that comes due the following month. The credit is applied to rent first, then utilities, then fees. A charge fully covered by your credit shows as settled with a "Covered by work-trade credit" note.

## Logging your hours

For each work session, submit the work date, the hours worked, and a short description of what you did — all three are required. Each entry starts as **pending** and only counts toward your credit once your landlord **approves** it. A rejected entry doesn't count, and may include a reason. Your log shows every entry with its status, and your progress view shows approved hours against your target plus roughly what percent of next month's bill is covered so far.

A practical tip: log hours as you work them and keep descriptions specific. Your landlord reviews each entry, and a clear description ("cleaned pool deck and skimmed pool, 2.5 hrs") gets approved faster than a vague one. If an entry sits pending or gets rejected and you're not sure why, talk to your landlord — they control approvals and the agreement terms.
