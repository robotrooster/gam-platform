title: Finding your way around the tenant portal
---
The GAM tenant portal is organized into these sections, which you reach from the navigation menu. These are the actual sections — if you're looking for something, it's in one of these:

- **Home** — your starting point when you log in, with an at-a-glance overview.
- **Lease** — your lease agreement: the terms, rent amount, dates, and policies. You can read it here and open or download a signed copy. (While your application is still in progress, you'll see an **Application** tab here instead; once you're approved it becomes your full portal.)
- **Payments** — pay your rent, see your payment history, and manage the bank account or card you pay with.
- **Communication** — one dashboard with tabs for everything you exchange with your landlord: **Maintenance** (file and track repair requests), **Inspections** (move-in, move-out, and periodic), **Entry Requests** (your landlord's requests to enter your unit), **Surveys**, **Documents** (your signed documents), and **My Walkthroughs** (the inspection walkthrough videos you've recorded).
- **Amenities** — browse and book the amenities your property offers (shown when your property has any).
- **Flex Advantage** — optional Flex products GAM is rolling out to tenants. You'll only see this where it's available to you — it isn't available to everyone yet.
- **Payouts** — appears once you have money on its way back to you, such as a deposit return.
- **Profile** — your account and profile settings, including your **password, two-factor authentication, and sign-in security**, and your **notification preferences** (turn email on or off per type; in-app alerts are always on).

Support isn't a sidebar section — it's the floating chat bubble on every page (this is where you are now). If you can't find something or aren't sure which section to use, just tell the support team what you're trying to do and they'll point you to the right place — or handle it for you directly.
