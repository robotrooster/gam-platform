GAM is the software platform you and your landlord both use to handle renting: paying rent, filing maintenance requests, signing documents, and keeping a clear record of all of it in one place.

## GAM is the platform, not your landlord

This distinction decides who can actually help you. GAM does not own the property, set your rent, write your lease, perform repairs, or make decisions about your unit. Those belong to your landlord and are governed by your lease and your local laws.

So your rent amount, due date, grace period and late fees come from your lease, which your landlord set — you can read yours any time under the Lease section. Repairs are your landlord's call; you start that by filing a maintenance request, which GAM routes to them.

## Three doors when you need help

- **This assistant** — the fastest one. It can look at your actual account, so you get your numbers rather than a general explanation.
- **GAM support** — platform and account problems the assistant can't resolve, or any time you'd rather talk to a person.
- **Your landlord** — anything about your home, your lease terms, your deposit, or a repair.

## What always goes to a person

Some things are handed to a human GAM Strategist no matter what:

- Moving or refunding money
- Account security, access, or sign-in problems

Just ask to be connected and we'll bring one in.
