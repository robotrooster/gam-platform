---
scope: tenant
title: Reporting a problem with a neighbour, a pet, parking, or the property
---

Not everything that goes wrong is a repair. If the trouble is someone else's
behaviour — noise at night, a dog barking, somebody parked in your space, smoke
or a smell drifting over, bins left out — that's a **complaint**, and it goes to
your landlord as a separate thing from maintenance.

You can raise one by telling the assistant in your own words. You don't need to
categorise it or use particular wording: "the people upstairs are up until 3am
every night" is enough. It's recorded against your unit and your lease, in the
words you used, and your landlord is notified.

## What GAM records it as

Every complaint is filed under one of these: **noise, neighbour, parking, pets,
smell, trash, property condition, harassment,** or **safety** — and **other**
when none of those fit. The category is just how it's sorted on your landlord's
list; it doesn't change how seriously it's treated, and it doesn't limit what
you can say.

If you mention the same problem again in the same conversation, it isn't filed
twice. A landlord reading the same complaint nine times learns nothing the first
one didn't already tell them.

## What happens next

Your complaint lands on your landlord's list as **open**. From there they can
mark it **reviewed** while they look into it, then **resolved** once it's dealt
with, or **dismissed** if they're not going to act on it. They can attach a note
explaining what they did.

**You won't see a status screen for it in your portal.** There isn't one — the
complaint goes to your landlord, and the update comes from them. If you want to
know where it got to, ask your landlord directly. Anyone telling you to "check
the status in your portal" is describing something that doesn't exist.

## When it's maintenance instead

If something in your unit is broken — a leak, a dead outlet, heating that won't
come on, a lock that doesn't catch — that's a **maintenance request**, not a
complaint, and it goes down a different path with its own status you *can*
follow. Report it as maintenance and it reaches whoever actually fixes things.

The rule of thumb: a complaint is about somebody's behaviour or the state of
shared space. A maintenance request is about something that needs repairing.

## Safety and emergencies

GAM is not an emergency service and nobody is watching your messages in real
time. If someone is in danger, if there's a fire, a gas smell, or a crime in
progress, **call your local emergency number first.** Report it here afterwards
so there's a record — but the call comes first.

Harassment and safety complaints are recorded like any other and go to your
landlord. If the situation involves your landlord, or you need it handled by
someone other than them, say so and you'll be put in touch with a person at GAM.
