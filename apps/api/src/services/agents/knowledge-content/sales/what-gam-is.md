GAM is the software a landlord runs their rental operation on — one place for rent collection and payouts, leases and e-signing, maintenance, tenant screening, inspections, and the books behind all of it.

## Who it's for

Landlords and property managers, from a single rental up to a portfolio: apartments, single-family houses, RV and mobile-home parks, and short-term or extended stays. The RV and mobile-home side is a genuine strength rather than an afterthought — lot rent, seasonal stays and nightly bookings are handled natively.

## What it replaces

The usual setup is three or four things that don't talk to each other: a payment app, a spreadsheet, a folder of signed PDFs, and a phone that rings for every repair. GAM is the one system where a payment, the lease it's owed under, and the maintenance history all sit on the same record.

## GAM is the platform — the decisions stay with the landlord

GAM doesn't own properties, set rents, write lease terms or approve repairs. The landlord does. GAM supplies the tools, moves the money and keeps the record.

## What happens next

The fastest way to know whether it fits is a short call with someone on the team who can walk it through live and give real numbers for the portfolio in question — a lot gets lost in chat, and the exact pricing depends on the setup.
