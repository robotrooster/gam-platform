title: What GAM costs
---
GAM's pricing is straightforward and built so you only pay for what you're actually using. Here's the honest shape of it — with the caveat that the exact number depends on your portfolio, your state, and which features you opt into, so a quick call with the team is how you get a real quote.

**Platform fee.** The baseline is **$2 per occupied unit per month** — and **vacant units are never charged**, so you're not paying for empty space. There's a **$10 per property monthly minimum**, so very small properties effectively pay a bit more per unit. Think of $2/unit as the anchor, not a universal price: larger portfolios can qualify for lower per-unit rates, and what you actually pay overall depends on what you enroll in beyond the core platform.

**What moves the number.** Several things are enrollment-based rather than baked in — you only pay for what you turn on:

- **Tenant screening** — background checks have a small per-screening fee on the landlord side, and the applicant covers the check itself. Some states cap or prohibit what applicants can be charged, which can shift part of that cost — it depends on where your properties are.
- **Optional programs** like **FlexVault** — GAM holding your tenant deposits in custody for you — change what a tailored quote looks like.
- **State-specific requirements** your properties are subject to can affect the picture too.

**Tenant payment processing.** When tenants pay electronically: bank (ACH) payments run **1% capped at $6**, cards run **3.25% + $0.26 per transaction**. Card fees are always covered by the tenant; for ACH, a per-property setting lets you decide whether the tenant covers it or you do. GAM never absorbs costs in a way that's hidden from you — you always see the breakdown.

**Getting paid.** Payouts to your bank run through Stripe, and the small per-account cost Stripe charges is absorbed into the platform fee — no separate line item.

So: $2 per occupied unit is the number to anchor on, it can come in lower for bigger portfolios, and the all-in picture depends on your state and what you opt into. For exact numbers tailored to your portfolio, the best path is a quick call with our team — I'm happy to set that up.
