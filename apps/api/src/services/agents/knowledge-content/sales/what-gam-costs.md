title: What GAM costs
---
GAM's pricing is straightforward and built so you only pay for what you're actually using. Here's the honest shape of it — with the caveat that the exact number depends on your portfolio, your state, and which features you opt into, so a quick call with the team is how you get a real quote.

**Platform fee.** The baseline is **$2 per occupied unit per month** — and **vacant units are never charged**, so you're not paying for empty space. There's a **$10 monthly minimum per payout account** — not per property, so several properties paying into the same bank account share one $10 floor between them. Only a very small portfolio effectively pays more per unit. Think of $2/unit as the anchor, not a universal price: larger portfolios can qualify for lower per-unit rates, and what you actually pay overall depends on what you enroll in beyond the core platform. And if you're switching over from somewhere else, you won't be charged the platform fee until you go live — so you're never paying GAM and your old platform at the same time during the move.

**What moves the number.** Several things are enrollment-based rather than baked in — you only pay for what you turn on:

- **Tenant screening** — where their state allows it, the applicant covers the whole check, so in the normal case screening costs you nothing. Some states cap or prohibit what applicants can be charged, and only then does part of that cost shift to you — it depends on where your properties are.
- **Optional programs** like **FlexVault** — turn your tenant security deposits over to GAM to hold in custody, and your per-unit platform fee comes down — can change what a tailored quote looks like.
- **State-specific requirements** your properties are subject to can affect the picture too.

**Tenant payment processing.** When tenants pay electronically: bank (ACH) payments are a **flat $6 per payment**, cards run **3.5% + $0.55 per transaction**. Card fees are always covered by the tenant; for ACH, a per-property setting lets you decide whether the tenant covers it or you do. GAM never absorbs costs in a way that's hidden from you — you always see the breakdown.

**Getting paid.** Payouts to your bank run through Stripe, and the small per-account cost Stripe charges is absorbed into the platform fee — no separate line item.

So: $2 per occupied unit is the number to anchor on, it can come in lower for bigger portfolios, and the all-in picture depends on your state and what you opt into. For exact numbers tailored to your portfolio, the best path is a quick call with our team — I'm happy to set that up.
