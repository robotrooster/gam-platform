## 2. Service Description

FlexPay is a **payment-date coordination subscription**. In these Subscription Terms:

- "**Monthly Bill**" means the bill the Platform issues to Subscriber for a calendar month under Subscriber's lease.
- "**Grace Period**" means the late-fee grace period in Subscriber's lease (five days when the lease sets none).
- "**Cover Day**" means the last day of the Grace Period of a Monthly Bill: the bill's due date plus the Grace Period, less one day, on the calendar of the property where Subscriber lives.
- "**Scheduled Pull Date**" means the first date on or after the Cover Day that falls on the pull day Subscriber chooses under Section 3.

**FlexPay pays the bill.** On the Cover Day of each Monthly Bill, FlexPay pays the Landlord, on Subscriber's behalf, every line of that Monthly Bill payable to the Landlord that is still open that day: rent, utilities, and fees on that month's bill (the "**Covered Lines**"). If a bill line is waiting on a scheduled retry of an earlier bank payment, FlexPay pays that line and the retry is called off, so Subscriber is never debited for it twice.

**FlexPay does not pay:**

- late fees already charged on the bill;
- balances carried from earlier months;
- a security deposit or any other refundable deposit;
- a home payment — a payment toward buying the home Subscriber lives in;
- GAM's own fees; or
- a line whose payment is already in progress on the Cover Day.

Those lines remain Subscriber's to pay under the lease. Credit saved on Subscriber's account is not spent by FlexPay; it stays available to Subscriber.

**Late fees.** Because the Covered Lines are paid before the Grace Period ends, the Landlord's late fee does not apply to them. While a line FlexPay does not pay stays open on the bill after the Grace Period, the Landlord's late-fee terms apply to the bill as the lease provides. FlexPay does not otherwise modify any Landlord remedy under the lease.

**GAM collects on the Scheduled Pull Date.** On the Scheduled Pull Date, GAM collects from Subscriber's Authorized Account, in a single ACH debit, the amount FlexPay paid for that month plus the Monthly Fee under Section 3 (and any other GAM-side balance under Section 5).

**A month Subscriber already paid.** If Subscriber pays the Monthly Bill before its Cover Day, FlexPay pays nothing that month, and GAM collects only the Monthly Fee on the Scheduled Pull Date.

**A second bill in the same month.** If a second Monthly Bill reaches its Cover Day in the same calendar month — for example, the first bill of a new lease of the same home that starts mid-month — before that month's collection is made, FlexPay pays it too, and the collection on the Scheduled Pull Date includes both.

**The Landlord.** The Landlord receives the Covered Lines on the Platform's regular payout schedule, as for any payment made through the Platform.

**Autopay is turned off.** Enrolling in FlexPay turns off any autopay Subscriber has set up on the Platform, because FlexPay pays the bill and GAM collects on the Scheduled Pull Date. If FlexPay ends, autopay stays off; GAM tells Subscriber, and Subscriber may turn autopay back on in the Platform.

FlexPay does not change the amount of rent or other charges under the lease.

**Changing the pull day.** Subscriber may change the pull day at any time through the in-platform FlexPay settings. The change takes effect for the next Monthly Bill; a collection already scheduled keeps its date. The Monthly Fee remains a flat $25.00 and does not change with the pull day.

### 4.1 Failed Collection — Retries

If Subscriber's bank returns a debit under this Section 4 for **insufficient funds** or **uncollected funds**, GAM tries the debit again **three (3) calendar days later**, and, if that try is also returned for one of those reasons, again three (3) calendar days after it — **at most two (2) retries** in all.

A debit returned because the **account is closed**, the account cannot be found, the account number is not valid, or the account holder told the bank the debit was not authorized is **never retried**.

**The Monthly Fee does not change on a retry.** The fee is a flat $25.00 and is not recalculated by the retry date. Each retry collects:

- **(a)** the amount FlexPay paid for that month (which does not change);
- **(b)** the flat **$25.00** Monthly Fee for that month, not re-priced;
- **(c)** the Stripe pass-through fee under Section 4.2 for every return of that collection so far — one after the first return, two after the second; and
- **(d)** any other GAM-side balance applied first under Section 5.

### 4.2 Stripe Pass-Through Fees

When a debit under this FlexPay subscription is returned, reversed, or disputed, Stripe charges GAM a per-event fee (currently $4.00 for a returned ACH debit). **Subscriber pre-authorizes the pass-through of those Stripe-charged fees to Subscriber, at GAM's actual cost, without markup by GAM.**

The pass-through fee is added to the retry under Section 4.1(c), or, when no retry follows, to Subscriber's GAM-side balance under Section 5.

Pass-through fees are **not** GAM service fees and are **not** a finance charge under Regulation Z. They are the actual cost of payment processing that GAM incurred and that Subscriber pre-authorizes to bear because the event causing the fee was attributable to Subscriber's payment activity. Stripe's current ACH-return / dispute / failure fee schedule is published at https://stripe.com/pricing and may be revised by Stripe from time to time; the schedule in effect on the date of each event governs that event's pass-through amount.

### 4.3 When a Collection Cannot Be Made — FlexPay Ends

**The last try fails.** When the last try of a collection is returned — the second retry, or the first try on an account that is never retried under Section 4.1 — FlexPay ends. Everything that collection did not collect — the amount FlexPay paid, the Monthly Fee, and the pass-through fees under Section 4.2 — becomes Subscriber's GAM-side balance under Section 5.

**A collection taken back.** A debit that settled and is later taken back by Subscriber's bank — for example, one the account holder reports as unauthorized, which can happen up to sixty (60) days after it settled — is treated the same way: FlexPay ends, and the amount taken back becomes Subscriber's GAM-side balance together with any return or dispute fee Stripe charges GAM, passed through at cost under Section 4.2.

**Waiting period.** After FlexPay ends under any paragraph above or under "Bank payments stopped" below, Subscriber may not enroll again for **ninety (90) days**. If it happens a **second time**, FlexPay ends for good and Subscriber may not enroll again.

**Bank payments stopped.** If bank payments are stopped on Subscriber's account after a returned payment, or Subscriber has no verified bank account on file, the collection is treated as the last try failing: FlexPay ends, the waiting period above applies, and the amount FlexPay paid with the Monthly Fee becomes Subscriber's GAM-side balance. If that happens before a Cover Day, FlexPay ends and does not pay that Monthly Bill, which remains Subscriber's to pay.

**A problem on GAM's side.** If a collection cannot be made because of a problem on GAM's side and not at Subscriber's bank, nothing is held against Subscriber: FlexPay does not end, no waiting period applies, and GAM makes the collection once the problem is fixed. If a problem on GAM's side keeps FlexPay from paying a Covered Line on time and the Landlord charges a late fee for it, GAM pays that late fee for Subscriber.

**Enrolling again.** Subscriber may enroll again only after any FlexPay amount in Subscriber's GAM-side balance has been collected, any waiting period has passed, and the conditions in Section 2.1 are met.

GAM tells Subscriber in the Platform and by email when FlexPay ends, why, what is still open with GAM, and when Subscriber may enroll again.
